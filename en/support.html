JIGSOA · HELP & INFORMATION

Support

Help with puzzles, purchases, and saved progress.

What can I play for free?

You can replay 10 fixed artworks at all three sizes: 10, 20, and 30 pieces. Hints, saved progress, and stories and looking activities after completion are included.

What does the Full Collection include?

One optional in-app purchase unlocks all 50 artworks and any artworks added in the future. There are no subscriptions or ads. Check the purchase screen for the current price.

I purchased the collection, but it is locked.

Connect to the internet and use the same Apple Account as the original purchase. Open Settings, choose the purchase and restoration option, and select Restore purchases.

Can I play offline?

Puzzle play works offline. Purchases, restoration, and opening museum websites require an internet connection.

Can I move my progress to another device?

Progress is stored on your device. The app does not provide its own progress sync or export feature. Deleting the app removes local progress, so please do not reinstall it before contacting support about a problem.

How do I change languages?

In iPad settings, change the app’s preferred language to Japanese or English. The location of this setting varies by operating-system version.

How do I report a problem?

Include your iPad model, iPadOS and app versions, artwork name, and the steps before the problem. Do not send passwords, payment-card details, or Apple verification codes.
