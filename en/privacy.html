JIGSOA · HELP & INFORMATION

Privacy Policy

Updated: 2026-11-14

Scope

This policy explains how the Jigsoa iPad app handles information.

Data sent to the developer

The app does not send puzzle progress or settings to developer-operated servers. It has no ads, advertising tracking, or third-party analytics SDKs. There is no account registration.

Information stored on your device

The app stores progress for each artwork and puzzle size, piece positions, and completion records and dates in its own storage. Sound, reduced-motion, and puzzle-guide preferences are stored in app preferences. These records let you resume puzzles and keep your settings.

Retention and deletion

App records are stored on your device. The app does not provide its own device-to-device sync. Using Delete App removes its local data; using Offload App may keep it. Data may be included in operating-system backups, which you manage through Apple settings. The app has no progress-export feature.

Purchases

Purchases and restoration use Apple’s App Store and StoreKit. The app verifies transaction information provided by Apple to determine access to the full collection. The app has no mechanism for sending your name, payment-card number, or billing address to the developer. Apple manages purchase history and refunds; deleting the app does not delete those records.

External links and Apple services

Choosing a museum artwork link opens an external website. Its operator may process information such as your IP address under its own policy. Please refer to Apple’s policies for information Apple handles in operating-system, diagnostics, and App Store services.

Support

If you contact support by email or another channel, your reply address and message are used to respond. Do not send passwords, payment-card details, or Apple verification codes. There is no support submission form inside the app.

Changes

If app features or information-handling practices change, this policy and its update date will be revised.
